Every conversation about abundance starts with a unit: **100 MWh per person per year**.

## Density is destiny

A uranium fuel pellet the size of a fingertip holds as much energy as a ton of coal.
That is why a plant needs trucks of fuel *per year*, not *per day*.

- Less land per kilowatt-hour
- Less material per kilowatt-hour
- Firm output, whatever the weather
